Why Contractors Trust tradecalc.net

Accurate material estimates are the foundation of every profitable project. Over-ordering wastes money. Under-ordering delays jobs. tradecalc.net gives contractors, estimators, and homeowners the precision they need before placing a single order.

HVAC Sizing Done Right

Undersizing or oversizing your HVAC system is one of the most expensive mistakes a contractor can make. Our BTU calculator accounts for room dimensions, ceiling height, insulation quality, and your regional climate zone to deliver accurate sizing recommendations. Our AC tonnage calculator translates those BTUs into the right unit size so you're never guessing.

Concrete & Material Estimation

Whether you're pouring a slab, setting posts, or forming footings, knowing exactly how many cubic yards of concrete to order saves time and money. Our concrete slab calculator converts length, width, and depth into cubic yards and bag counts. The sonotube calculator handles cylindrical pour volumes for post footings and deck supports.

Landscaping Material Calculations

Ordering the right amount of gravel or mulch is critical for landscaping bids. Too little and you're making an extra delivery run. Too much and you're absorbing the cost. Our gravel calculator gives you cubic yards and tons, while our mulch calculator provides both cubic yards and bag counts based on the depth you need.

Flooring & Tile Estimation

Tile installation requires precise waste factor calculations. Cut tiles around edges, corners, and fixtures add up fast. Our tile calculator factors in room size, tile dimensions, and your chosen waste percentage to tell you exactly how many tiles to order. The flooring waste calculator adjusts recommendations based on installation pattern — diagonal cuts require significantly more material.

Roofing Calculations Made Simple

Roof pitch affects everything from material quantity to labor time. A steeper pitch requires more shingles per square foot of floor area and takes longer to install safely. Our roof pitch calculator instantly converts rise-over-run measurements into pitch notation, degrees, and the multiplier factor you need to calculate actual roof surface area.

Paint Quantity Estimation

Paint coverage varies by brand, surface texture, and number of coats. Our paint calculator accounts for wall dimensions, door and window deductions, and number of coats to give you an accurate gallon estimate. Stop buying too much — or running out mid-job.

Reducing Waste and Saving Money

Material waste is one of the largest controllable costs on any construction or renovation project. Studies suggest that material waste accounts for 10–15% of project costs on average. Accurate pre-job estimation using the right calculators can cut that waste significantly, improving your margins and your reputation for professional project management.

  • Concrete: Ordering 5–10% extra is standard to account for spillage and variations in sub-base depth
  • Tile: Add 10% waste for straight patterns, 15% for diagonal or herringbone
  • Gravel: Material settles after delivery — order 10% extra for compacted applications
  • Paint: Textured surfaces absorb more paint — reduce the coverage rate by 10–15%
  • Mulch: 3 inches of depth is the sweet spot for weed suppression without smothering plants
