About tradecalc.net

Professional-grade trade calculators — free for contractors, builders, and homeowners. Built with accuracy and simplicity at the core.

Our Mission

tradecalc.net exists to give every contractor, tradesperson, and homeowner access to the same professional-grade estimation tools that used to require expensive software or years of field experience to master. We believe that accurate material estimation should be fast, free, and accessible to anyone with an internet connection.

Whether you're a licensed HVAC technician sizing a split system, a tile contractor quoting a bathroom renovation, or a homeowner planning a weekend project, our calculators deliver results you can rely on — in seconds, with no sign-up required.

What We Build

tradecalc.net provides a growing library of calculators organized by trade. Each calculator is designed around the specific formulas, standards, and industry practices used by working professionals:

HVAC Calculators

Our HVAC tools help size heating and cooling systems correctly. The BTU Calculator accounts for room size, ceiling height, insulation quality, and climate zone — the four factors that matter most in real-world load calculations. The AC Tonnage Calculator converts BTU requirements to equipment size. The CFM Calculator determines proper airflow based on room volume and air changes per hour. All calculations are grounded in ASHRAE guidelines and industry best practices.

Concrete Calculators

Concrete is unforgiving — you can't easily order more mid-pour, and over-ordering is expensive waste. Our Concrete Slab Calculator handles length, width, and depth in any unit combination to output cubic yards and bag counts. The Sonotube Calculator does the same for cylindrical concrete pier footings, a common foundation type for decks and posts.

Landscaping Calculators

Landscaping material calculations require matching material density to volume needs. Our Gravel Calculator accounts for different gravel types (pea gravel, crushed stone, decomposed granite) with appropriate density factors to give accurate tonnage and weight estimates. The Mulch Calculator converts area and desired depth to cubic yards and bag quantities.

Flooring Calculators

Flooring estimation is where material waste makes the biggest difference in project profitability and homeowner satisfaction. Our Tile Calculator handles any tile size and room dimension combination, with adjustable waste factors for different installation patterns. The Flooring Waste Calculator covers all major flooring types — hardwood, laminate, vinyl plank, carpet, tile, and cork — with pattern-specific waste percentages based on industry standards.

Roofing Calculators

Roof pitch is the foundation of every roofing estimate. Our Roof Pitch Calculator converts rise and run measurements into pitch ratio, angle in degrees, slope multiplier, and slope category — giving contractors and homeowners everything they need to understand roof geometry, verify material compatibility, and calculate actual surface area from footprint measurements.

Painting Calculators

Paint quantity estimation seems simple until you account for coats, doors, windows, and coverage rate variation by surface type. Our Paint Coverage Calculator handles all of these variables to deliver precise gallon estimates and recommended purchase quantities — so you buy the right amount and avoid costly re-trips to the paint counter.

Our Approach to Accuracy

Every calculator on tradecalc.net is built from the same formulas and constants used by industry professionals. We research manufacturer specifications, industry association guidelines (ASHRAE, TCNA, NRCA, APA), and building codes to ensure our calculations match what experienced tradespeople know from the field.

That said, our calculators provide estimates for planning and bidding purposes. They are excellent tools for quick job costing, material procurement planning, and homeowner education. For permitted construction, engineered systems, or any situation where under-specification would create a safety or code issue, we always recommend verifying with a licensed professional and following local codes.

Who Uses tradecalc.net

Our users range from licensed contractors using our tools for quick bid estimates in the field, to first-time homeowners trying to understand how much material their renovation will require. We've designed each tool to work for both audiences — providing the professional-level inputs that contractors need while remaining clear enough for anyone to use without a trade background.

  • HVAC contractors — Quick system sizing verification before pulling permits
  • Flooring installers — Material procurement and customer-facing estimates
  • Roofers — Pitch calculation and material quantity verification
  • Painters — Accurate gallon estimates for professional bids
  • Concrete contractors — Pour volume and bag count verification
  • Homeowners — Planning DIY projects and understanding contractor quotes
  • Building managers — Evaluating renovation scope and material costs
  • Students and apprentices — Learning trade math and estimation fundamentals

Privacy and Data

tradecalc.net does not collect or store any data you enter into our calculators. All calculations run entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to our servers. We may use anonymous analytics to understand which tools are most popular and how users navigate the site, but we never collect personally identifiable information without explicit consent.
