Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. We believe in straightforward privacy. Here's exactly what we collect and why.

Overview

tradecalc.net ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at tradecalc.net (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our Site and use our calculator tools. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

The short version: Our calculators run entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data you enter into our calculator inputs. The numbers you type stay on your device.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Calculator Inputs — Not Collected

All calculation logic on tradecalc.net runs client-side in your web browser using JavaScript. When you enter room dimensions, material quantities, or any other values into our calculators, that data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not store, log, or have access to any values you enter into our calculator tools.

1.2 Analytics Data

We may use anonymized analytics services to understand how visitors use our Site. This may include:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Which calculators are used most frequently
  • General geographic region (country or state level — not precise location)
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile) and operating system
  • Referring website (how you found tradecalc.net)
  • Browser type and version

This data is aggregated and anonymized — it cannot be used to identify individual visitors. It helps us understand which tools are most valuable and how to improve the site experience.

1.3 Automatically Collected Technical Data

Like all websites, our hosting servers may automatically collect standard technical log data when you visit, including:

  • IP address (used to deliver web content to your browser; may be logged briefly by our hosting provider)
  • Date and time of the request
  • The URL requested
  • HTTP status code

Server logs are retained for a limited period for security and operational purposes and are not used to build profiles of individual visitors.

1.4 Contact Information

If you contact us via email or any future contact form, we will collect the information you choose to provide (such as your name and email address) solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not add contact information to mailing lists without explicit consent.

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser to remember preferences and track usage. tradecalc.net may use the following types of cookies:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic functionality of the Site. They do not collect personal information and cannot be disabled without affecting Site functionality.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

If we use a third-party analytics service (such as Google Analytics), it may set cookies to track anonymized page visits and usage patterns. These cookies do not contain personally identifiable information. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

2.3 Advertising Cookies

tradecalc.net may in the future display advertising through third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense. Advertising networks use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our Site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through your browser settings. You can set your browser to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being set. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some websites, though it will have minimal impact on tradecalc.net's core calculator functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Fonts

Our Site uses Google Fonts to display the Inter typeface. When your browser loads our pages, it makes a request to Google's servers to download the font files. Google may collect limited data about this request (such as IP address) pursuant to its own Privacy Policy. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Google AdSense (Future)

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. When AdSense is active, Google uses cookies and other technologies to show you ads that are relevant to you. The data collected by Google for advertising purposes is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3.3 Content Delivery Networks

Our Site may use content delivery network (CDN) services to serve assets efficiently. These services may log technical request data as described in Section 1.3 above.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used to:

  • Operate and maintain the tradecalc.net website
  • Understand which calculators and features are most valuable to users
  • Improve calculator accuracy, usability, and content quality
  • Detect and address technical issues or security concerns
  • Respond to user inquiries and support requests

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal data from calculator usage, there is no user data to retain or delete. Anonymized analytics data is retained for up to 26 months to identify usage trends over time. Server log data is typically retained for 30–90 days for security purposes.

6. Children's Privacy

tradecalc.net is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. Because we do not collect personal data through calculator usage, most data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) are inherently satisfied. If you have submitted information via a contact form or email, you may request that we delete that information by contacting us.

Residents of the European Economic Area (EEA) and California may have additional rights under GDPR and CCPA respectively. For questions about these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we process, including using HTTPS encryption for all Site traffic. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method is 100% secure. We encourage users not to transmit sensitive personal or financial information via our contact channels.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
